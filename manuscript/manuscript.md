### The QUIPS Tool

The Quality In Prognosis Studies (QUIPS) tool [@Hayden2013] consists of six domains for evaluating risk of bias in prognosis studies:

1. **Study Participation** - Assessment of study sampling and representativeness
2. **Study Attrition** - Evaluation of loss to follow-up
3. **Prognostic Factor Measurement** - Quality of predictor measurement
4. **Outcome Measurement** - Quality of outcome assessment
5. **Study Confounding** - Adequacy of confounder adjustment
6. **Statistical Analysis and Reporting** - Appropriateness of statistical methods
